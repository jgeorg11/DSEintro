### Background

My academic career has followed a nontraditional trajectory. After high school, it was assumed that I would follow in the footsteps of my father and grandfather, both of whom attended the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and became dentists without taking breaks in their education. My uncle also attended UTK before becoming an orthopedic surgeon. Their experiences created additional pressure for me to pursue a similar path. However, I quickly realized that dentistry was not the career I wanted. Rather than continue investing time and money in a degree without a clear goal, I stepped away from college and joined the military while deciding on a career path.

Once I discovered my interest in information technology, I resumed my education and earned a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology Security while serving in the military. I continued my education while working at Microsoft, returning to school for a master’s degree in data analytics shortly after the birth of my first daughter. I am now considering continuing that academic journey through a PhD program, with the goal of contributing to the cutting edge of quantum technology or embodied AI through robotics. Through DSE 511, I hope to deepen my understanding of data science research methods, strengthen my ability to analyze and communicate findings, and apply those skills to meaningful research problems involving artificial intelligence, national laboratories, and beyond. In a field that never stops evolving, I am most excited to begin thinking about what comes next rather than merely learning what is next.
